Guess the Year

How to Play

You'll be shown a historical event. Use the slider — or the − and + buttons — to set the year you think it happened, then press Guess.

The colour of each result tells you how close you were — use it to guide your next guess!

Classic Mode

How to Play

You have 3 guesses per round. After each wrong guess, a new event from the same year appears as an extra clue.

The fewer guesses you use, the more points you earn:
200 pts — correct on 1st try
100 pts — correct on 2nd try
50 pts — correct on 3rd try
1–25 pts — wrong all 3 tries but within 25 years (proximity bonus)

The game lasts 5 rounds. Aim for the highest score and top the leaderboard!

Endless Mode

How to Play

You have only one attempt per question. Guess correctly and you score a point and move to the next event. Guess wrong and the game is over!

Choose your difficulty before you start:
Easy — guess within ±10 years
Medium — guess within ±5 years
Hard — exact year only
